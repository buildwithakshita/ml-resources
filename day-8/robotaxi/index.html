How the mini robotaxi learns to drive

  1. It sees. Each car has 5 sensors that measure how far the edge of the lane is: far right, right, ahead, left, far left.
  2. It decides. A tiny neural network, written from scratch, turns those 5 numbers into two decisions: how much to steer and how fast to go.
  3. It crashes. A lot. Generation 1 has random brains, so almost every car drives straight off the road.
  4. The best ones pass it on. The cars that got furthest have their brains copied with tiny random changes. No one tells them the right answer. Like evolution, generation after generation, they figure out how to drive the whole block.

Then try Robotaxi mode: the trained brain drives pickups and drop-offs on its own. Works offline once loaded, and remembers your progress on this device.
